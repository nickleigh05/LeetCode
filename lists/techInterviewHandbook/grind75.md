# Grind 75

The default Grind 75 plan: 8 weeks at 8 hours a week. Grind 75 is the successor to Blind 75, made by the same author and updated with newer questions and a better spread of topics. Work through it one week at a time. The weeks go from easy to hard: the early weeks are mostly Easy questions and the later weeks are Medium and Hard.

**75 problems · 24 Easy · 42 Medium · 9 Hard · 8 weeks · ~8 hrs/week**

Source: [Tech Interview Handbook — Grind 75](https://www.techinterviewhandbook.org/grind75). Weeks are scheduled the same way the site does it: questions are sorted by difficulty and packed into weekly time budgets using the site's per-question time estimates. 🔒 = LeetCode Premium.
